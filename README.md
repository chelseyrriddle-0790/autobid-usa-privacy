# autobid-usa-privacy

Privacy Policy for Autobid USA

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Autobid USA ("we," "our," or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, and protected when you use the Autobid USA mobile application.

Autobid USA provides a searchable directory of approximately 991 auto auctions throughout the United States, including auction locations, contact information, websites, auction schedules, and dealer-only or public access information when available.

2. Information We Collect

Autobid USA does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to browse auction listings.

Location Information

When you choose the "Find Auctions Near Me" feature, the App may request permission to access your device's location.

Location information is used to identify nearby auto auctions and calculate distances based on your selected search radius.

Location access is optional. You may deny or revoke location permission through your device settings.

Search Information

The App allows users to search and filter auctions by state, city, distance, and other available criteria.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by the App is used to:

- Display auto auctions relevant to your search.
- Identify auctions near your location.
- Calculate approximate distances to auction locations.
- Provide auction contact information, schedules, and website links.
- Support the App's search and filtering features.

4. Location Data

Autobid USA requests location access only when needed for location-based features.

The App does not require continuous background location tracking.

Location information is used to provide nearby auction search results.

5. Third-Party Websites

Autobid USA provides links to independently operated auto auction websites.

When you visit these websites, their own privacy policies and terms of service apply.

Autobid USA is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

6. Data Sharing

Autobid USA does not sell users' personal information.

Some application functions may depend on external services or websites. Information processed by these services is subject to their applicable privacy practices.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by the App. However, no electronic system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Autobid USA is intended for individuals seeking information about automobile auctions and is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Data Retention and User Choices

Users may disable location access at any time through their device settings.

The App does not require account registration. Users may discontinue use of the App and uninstall it at any time.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, privacy practices, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
