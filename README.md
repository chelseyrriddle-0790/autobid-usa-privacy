# autobid-usa-privacy
Privacy Policy for Autobid USA

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Autobid USA ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application that helps users discover automobile auctions throughout the United States.

This Privacy Policy explains how Autobid USA accesses, uses, and handles information when you use the application.

Contact: Autobidusa.app@gmail.com

2. Information We Access

Location Information

Autobid USA may request access to your device's approximate or precise location when you select the "Find Auctions Near Me" feature.

Location information is used to identify nearby automobile auctions and calculate approximate distances from your location.

Location access is optional. You can browse auction listings without enabling location services.

Autobid USA does not request background location permission.

Search Information

Users can search for auctions by state, city, and other available filters. Search selections are used to display relevant auction results.

Account Information

Autobid USA does not require users to register an account, sign in, or provide their name, email address, or telephone number to use the auction directory.

3. How Information Is Used

Autobid USA uses information to:

- Display automobile auction listings.
- Find auctions near your current location.
- Calculate approximate distances to auctions.
- Filter auctions by location and available criteria.
- Retrieve auction information from external websites.
- Provide links to auction websites and contact information.
- Support vehicle information lookup features.

4. Location Services

Autobid USA uses Android location services to obtain your location when you request nearby auction results.

The application may also use Android's geocoding services to identify cities or geographic locations. Depending on your device and service provider, geocoding may involve communication with an external service.

You can revoke location permission at any time through your Android device settings.

Autobid USA does not require continuous background location tracking.

5. Third-Party Services and Websites

Autobid USA may communicate with third-party services, including:

- Android and Google location services, for location-related functionality.
- Android geocoding services, for geographic searches.
- Automobile auction websites, for retrieving publicly available auction information.
- The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) API, for vehicle-related information.

When the application connects to an external website or service, that provider may receive technical information associated with the connection, such as your IP address.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and practices.

6. Analytics and Advertising

The reviewed version of Autobid USA does not include an active Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics integration.

The application does not require users to provide personal information for advertising purposes.

If analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

7. Data Storage and Retention

Autobid USA uses a local database to store auction-related information on your device.

Certain application data may be included in Android device backup services, depending on device settings and backup configuration.

Users can revoke location permissions or uninstall the application at any time.

Third-party services may maintain their own records of requests made to them, according to their respective privacy policies.

8. Data Sharing

Autobid USA does not sell users' personal information.

Information necessary to provide certain features may be processed by device services or third-party providers, as described in this policy.

Autobid USA does not require users to create accounts or submit personal contact information to access auction listings.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

However, no internet connection, electronic storage system, or third-party service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Autobid USA is intended for individuals interested in automobile auctions and is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You may:

- Deny location permission when requested.
- Revoke location permission through Android settings.
- Browse auction listings without using the nearby-search feature.
- Uninstall the application to discontinue its use.
- Contact us with questions about information handled by the application.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
